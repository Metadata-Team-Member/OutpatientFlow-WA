What the target indicators mean

The red dashed line in each queue lane is the timeframe in the WA Health Non-Admitted Data Collection Business Rules (July 2026) §8.1: Urgent (priority 1) within 30 days, Semi Urgent (priority 2) within 90, Not Urgent (priority 3) within 365; the same three measures appear in §10.2.2. The green / amber / red percentage is the share of first attended appointments in the last 12 months that happened within that timeframe, counting from the referral received date. Only an attended first appointment ends the wait: a non-attendance (DNA), a cancellation, a booking, a chart-only entry or an MCC event does not (§10.2.3 to §10.2.7).

Referrals with the reason Technical transfer (called Ongoing patient management before 1 July 2026) are left out of that measure, as the Data Dictionary excludes them from first-attended waiting-time reporting. Percentages come from synthetic data and say nothing about real performance.

All activity (people, referrals, appointments, waits, counts) is invented by a random-number generator so the design can be tested before any real extract is requested. Nothing here should be quoted as a finding about WA Health, any service or any person. Aligned to the WA Health Non-Admitted Data Collection Data Specifications, July 2026 (V003), Business Rules, July 2026, Data Dictionary 2026 and Establishment List, May 2026 (v0.1); the establishments shown are exactly those marked “Reports to NADC” = Y. How to read this

  • One marker = one referral. Every marker is the same friendly, gender-neutral shape with a leaf sprout: no hair, no clothes, and the same size for everyone. The marker stands for a referral, not a person.
  • Rings = service use. The rings on the ground around a marker show the number of attended outpatient events: no ring means none so far; more and wider rings mean more attended events (we count attended events, including earlier ones in the 24 months before the playback starts). Lower service use and higher service use only describe a count of events. They do not say whether the care was too little or too much for that person.
  • Panel 1, the map. Real WA health regions (34 districts → 10 regions) with a Perth metro inset. Coloured dots are WA Health establishments (exactly the sites marked “Reports to NADC” = Y in the Establishment List, May 2026), sized by beds and coloured by Health Service Provider. A referral appears at the centre of its health district. A dot pulses when an appointment is attended there.
  • Panel 2, the queue. One lane per referral priority. The red dashed line is the Business Rules §8.1 timeframe (30 / 90 / 365 days). Markers past it get a red outline and a concerned expression.
  • Panel 3, outpatient services. One block per Health Service Provider, one door per clinic category. Attended appointment: the marker goes through the door and gains a ring. Telehealth or phone: a blue beam, no walking. Did not attend (DNA): the marker walks to the door and turns away, with the non-attendance reason shown. Cancelled: a "✕ cancelled" note with the cancellation reason, blue if the hospital cancelled and amber if the patient did not attend. Chart only / MCC (non-client events): a small grey note; the patient is not present, so the marker does not move and keeps waiting (Business Rules §10.2.7).
  • Establishment spotlight (last block): hover or click a dot to see one establishment, or click a row in the "most attended" list to filter to it.
  • Referrals from inside the hospital and technical transfer referrals start already in care and are left out of "seen within target".
  • Markers shown displays a sample so the screen stays readable. Numbers and charts use every invented patient.

What real data we'd need

This prototype runs on invented data shaped like the WA Non-Admitted Data Collection (NADC) extract defined in the Data Specifications, July 2026 (Appendix B). To swap in real data, the key items are:

  • Referral records: establishment code, referral_account_number, referral_category_code, referral_received_date, referral_priority_code, referral_reason_code, referral_source_code, referral_status_code, referral_closed_date, referral_reason_closure_code.
  • Appointment records: appointment_account_number, appointment_date, appointment_type_code, appointment_attendance_code, appointment_status_code, appointment_outcome_code, appointment_nonattend_reas_code, appointment_cancellation_code and date, appointment_delivery_mode_code, appointment_session_type_code, appointment_client_type_code, appointment_care_type_code, and the clinic fields clinic_category_code, clinic_nmds_tier1_code and clinic_tier2_code.
  • De-identified person fields (not in the extract, so they need a separate request): a project-specific person key, age, Aboriginal status (not shown or used in this prototype), and residential area (postcode or SA2) to place people on the map.
  • Probably not in this collection (to confirm with the custodian): people who never reached the hospital (not referred, not accepted, or still with the Central Referral Service before a hospital receives the referral).
  • Governance: approval through the WA Health data custodian / Data Linkage Services, small-number suppression, and Aboriginal data governance.
