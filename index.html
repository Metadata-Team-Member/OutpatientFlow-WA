SYNTHETIC EXAMPLE DATA

Everything on this page is invented by a random-number generator so the design can be tested before any real extract is requested. Hospital names are generic labels. No real patient, hospital or WA Health number appears here, and nothing here should be quoted as a finding. Designed around the field names and concepts of the WA Department of Health Non-Admitted Data Collection.

Data requirements log · Dictionary mapping · Source on GitHub

How to read this

  • One character = one referral episode. Soft, neutral little beans with a sprout on top: no gender, no hair, no clothes.
  • Size = care received. Thin means few or no attended outpatient events. Each attended appointment makes the character larger (we count attended events, including earlier ones in the last 24 months).
  • Outside (left): referrals from GPs, specialists and others arrive from their region of Western Australia and wait in the queue. The further right they drift, the closer they get to (and then past) the indicative wait target for their urgency category.
  • Inside (right): six invented hospital sites with clinic doors. Attended appointments make characters walk in through the right door and come out bigger. People who do not attend (DNA) turn away. Telehealth appointments are shown as a signal beam.
  • Referrals from inside the hospital (emergency department, inpatient ward, same hospital outpatient) start inside the network, which is part of why they tend to be seen sooner.
  • Crowd size shows a sample so the screen stays readable. The numbers and charts always use every invented patient.

What real data we'd need

This prototype runs on invented data shaped like the WA Non-Admitted Data Collection. To swap in real data, the key items are:

  • Referral records: referral source, priority, received date, discharge/closure date and reason, clinic category, establishment.
  • Appointment records: appointment date, attendance (attended / did not attend / did not wait), outcome, delivery mode (in person, phone, video), new vs follow-up, clinic and establishment.
  • De-identified person fields: a project-specific person key, age, Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander status, residential postcode or SA2.
  • Not in this collection: people who never reached the hospital (referrals not yet received, not accepted, or never sent). That "unmet demand" needs another source.
  • Governance: approval through the WA Health data custodian / Data Linkage Services, small-number suppression, and Aboriginal data governance.
