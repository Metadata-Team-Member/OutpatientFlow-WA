What the target indicators mean

The red dashed line in each queue lane is the timeframe in the WA Health Non-Admitted Patient Activity Data Business Rules §8.1 (p.14): priority 1 within 30 days, priority 2 within 90, priority 3 within 365. The green / amber / red percentage is the share of first attended appointments in the last 12 months that happened within that timeframe, counting from the referral received date.

Ongoing patient management (OPM) referrals are left out of that measure, as the Business Rules exclude them from new-appointment wait reporting (§8.3.1). Mapping the Urgent / Semi-urgent / Not urgent codes to priorities 1 / 2 / 3 is this prototype's reading and is listed as an open question for the data custodian. Percentages come from synthetic data and say nothing about real performance.

All activity (people, referrals, appointments, waits, counts) is invented by a random-number generator so the design can be tested before any real extract is requested. Nothing here should be quoted as a finding about WA Health. The establishment list and boundaries are reference data from the WA Government's SLIP / data.wa.gov.au HEALTH layers (hospitals, nursing posts, health districts and regions); field names and code sets follow the WA Health NAPDC data specification, business rules and dictionary. How to read this

  • One character = one referral episode. Soft, neutral little beans with a sprout on top: no gender, no hair, no clothes.
  • Size = care received. Thin means few or no attended events. Each attended appointment makes the character larger (we count attended events, including earlier ones in the 24 months before the playback starts).
  • Panel 1, the map. Real WA health regions (34 districts → 10 regions) with a Perth metro inset. Coloured dots are reporting establishments, sized by beds and coloured by Health Service Provider. A referral appears at the centre of its health district. A dot pulses when an appointment is attended there.
  • Panel 2, the queue. One lane per referral priority. The red dashed line is the Business Rules §8.1 timeframe (30 / 90 / 365 days). Characters who pass it look worried.
  • Panel 3, inside the network. One block per Health Service Provider, one door per clinic category. Attended appointment: the character goes through the door and comes out bigger. Telehealth or phone: a blue beam, no walking. Did not attend: walks to the door, turns away, with the non-attendance reason shown. Cancelled: a "✕ cancelled" note with the cancellation reason, blue if the hospital cancelled and amber if the patient did.
  • Establishment spotlight (last block): hover or click a dot to see one establishment, or click a row in the "busiest" list to filter to it.
  • Referrals from inside the hospital and ongoing patient management start inside the network and are left out of "seen within target".
  • Crowd size shows a sample so the screen stays readable. Numbers and charts use every invented patient.

Rules in the simulation

Rule text is summarised from the WA Health Non-Admitted Patient Activity Data Business Rules (v1.0, MP 0164/21) and the matching Data Specification. The official PDFs could not be downloaded by this project's tools, so these were read from a third-party mirror of the same documents and need confirming against the originals. Details: BUSINESS-RULES-SUMMARY.md.

RuleSourceIn this simulation
An appointment is attended when an appointment outcome is allocated.BR §5.1 p.3Outcome allocated = attended. A did-not-attend is modelled as a non-attendance reason with a blank outcome. This is an assumption (open question).
Priority 1 seen within 30 days, priority 2 within 90, priority 3 within 365.BR §8.1 p.14Queue lanes and the red target line. Urgent / Semi-urgent / Not urgent are read as priority 1 / 2 / 3 (assumption).
Ongoing patient management (OPM) only after a first attended appointment; excluded from new-appointment wait reporting.BR §8.3, §8.3.1 pp.15-16About 8% of referrals are OPM and start inside the network. They are excluded from the "seen within target" and median-wait measures. They are not linked to an earlier referral.
Transfer: the original received date is kept; the original referral is closed "Transfer and close"; the new one is OPM and its first appointment is Follow-up.BR §8.4 p.16"Transfer and close" appears as a closure reason, but the linked second referral is not generated (simplified).
Closure reasons and auditing of long-waiting open referrals (the "Audit" closure).BR §8.5 p.17Closure codes TRE, DIP, PDT, TFC, DEC and AUD are used. No audit process is simulated; AUD is just a random closure.
Referral and appointment clinic category must match.BR (referral rules)Every appointment carries the referral's clinic category.
One open referral per category per condition.BR (referral rules)Each synthetic person has one referral, so the rule is trivially met.
Appointment cancellation, with a reason code and date.Spec elements 48-50, App. BA cancelled appointment has outcome CAN and a reason, grouped by us as hospital / patient (our grouping, not official).
Delivery mode: client present, group, telephone, video, telehealth at a WA Health site, telehealth with support clinician (and more).Spec App. BSix modes used. Telephone and the telehealth modes are drawn as a beam and counted as remote.
Daily and monthly extracts, file naming, record types REF / APP / RES / CAN.Spec v1.0The sample CSVs use Spec-style column names. Extract timing is not simulated.

Not simulated: validation rules, change types (insert / update), reschedules, appointment-level wait for follow-ups, referral transfer chains, and the Central Referral Service pathway.

What real data we'd need

This prototype runs on invented data shaped like the WA Non-Admitted Patient Activity collection. To swap in real data, the key items are:

  • Referral records: Establishment Code, Referral Account Number, Referral Received Date, Referral Priority Code, Referral Source Code, Referral Reason Code, Referral Category Code, Referral Closed Date and Reason for Closure Code.
  • Appointment records: Appointment Account Number, Appointment Date, Appointment Outcome Code, Non-Attendance Reason Code, Cancellation Code and Date, Delivery Mode, Appointment Type (new / follow-up), Clinic Category and Clinic Title.
  • De-identified person fields: a project-specific person key, age, Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander status, residential area (postcode or SA2) to place people on the map.
  • Not in this collection: people who never reached the hospital (not referred, not accepted, or in a referral service queue before the hospital receives it).
  • Governance: approval through the WA Health data custodian / Data Linkage Services, small-number suppression, and Aboriginal data governance.
