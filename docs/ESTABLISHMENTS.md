# Establishments in the simulation: Reports to NADC = Y (official List, May 2026)

**125 establishments.** The simulation's reporting sites are *exactly* the establishments with **Reports to NADC = Y** in the WA Health *Establishment List*, May 2026 (v0.1), as supplied to this project by its user. Generated by `tools/build_establishments_doc.mjs` from `data/reference/establishments.json` (built by `tools/build_establishments.mjs` from `data/reference/official_nadc_reporting_2026-05.json`). Sources are in [SOURCES.md](SOURCES.md).

## What is official and what is not

| Field | Where it comes from | Official? |
|---|---|---|
| **Code**, **Name**, **Reports to NADC = Y** | Establishment List May 2026, "Establishment List, Reporting" table | **Yes** |
| **Type** (AH, NP, OH, RC, DS ...) and **MPS** flag | Same document: the Hospital Morbidity System Codes table (Est type) and Appendix D (Multi-Purpose Services) | **Yes** (122 of 125 codes appear in that table; 3 do not and show "none") |
| **HSP** (Health Service Provider) | **Not in the List.** From the SLIP HEALTH-001 geographic service, with overrides from the Department of Health Central Referral Service page (see "Sites needing a note") | **No** |
| **Region, district, beds, suburb** | **Not in the List.** SLIP HEALTH-001 / HEALTH-002 | **No** |
| **Latitude / longitude** | **Not in the List.** SLIP HEALTH-001, or OpenStreetMap for the few that SLIP does not have | **No** |

* The List's Reporting table has **191 rows**; **125** are marked Reports to NADC = Y and **66** are not (the document does not say where those report; earlier search extracts showed day surgeries, hospices, dialysis centres and private hospitals among them). The rows that are not flagged are *not* in the simulation and are not reproduced here.
* **How the Y flags were read (be aware).** The PDF's text layer does not keep column positions for the Read tool, so a Y cannot be assigned to the NADC column from that alone. The flags come from a column-aware text extract of the same WA Health publication, returned through a search index; its edition is not stated. Its counts were cross-checked against the user-supplied May 2026 PDF (125 NADC = Y). **8 rows are inferred** rather than read directly (marked ‡ below): 0249 Wyndham Hospital; 0251 York Hospital; 0359 Yalgoo Health Centre; 0433 Warren Hospital; 0434 Wickepin Health Centre; 0436 Williams Health Centre; 0437 Wongan Hills Hospital; 0438 Wyalkatchem-Koorda and Districts Hospital. Please check those against the original.
* Where an official name differs from the SLIP name, the **official name is used**; the SLIP name is kept in the data as `slipName`.
