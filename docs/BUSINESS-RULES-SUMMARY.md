# Business rules summary (plain English)

**Source.** *Non-Admitted Data Collection Business Rules*, **July 2026** (BR), with the *Data Specifications July 2026* (Spec) and *Data Dictionary 2026* (DD); all supplied by the user (see [SOURCES.md](SOURCES.md)). This page paraphrases; it is not a copy. Section numbers are the documents' own. Page numbers are given only where they were recorded (BR Appendix A pp.40-43, Appendix D p.49; DD pages as shown); other BR rules are cited by section.

**Legend for the last column.** ✅ implemented in the simulation. ◑ simplified (what we do instead is stated). ✖ not simulated (out of scope or no data). Everything simulated is applied to **synthetic** data only.

## Conflicts between the sim's concept and the documents (honest list)

* **Adult psychiatry is out of scope** (BR §6.11, §10.1.1). The earlier sim had a PSY category; it was **removed**.
* **Community and home care are standard exclusions** (BR §10.1.1), yet the NADC establishment list includes referral-code / community-style sites (types RC, OH). The sim draws them as small clinics; whether their activity is outpatient or primary health is not stated in the documents.
* **HIT and RSH are "not to be reported"** (BR App A), yet establishment 652 (Silver Chain Hospital at Home) reports to the NADC.
* **Private hospitals report** (602, 612, 629, 6007) while BR excludes "services delivered by private hospitals to private patients". The documents do not reconcile the two for us.
* **The sim treats the NADC as showing a "waiting" crowd.** The NADC records referrals and appointments (the Spec has an Appointment Status FU = future appointment), but "waiting position" and booking offers are **not** in the collection; our "waiting" is *derived* (BR §10.2) from received date, status and first attended appointment.
* **The Establishment List has no HSP, region or coordinates.** Those come from SLIP and the CRS page.
* **Outcome is mandatory in the Spec (App B p.24) but not used for a DNA (BR §9.3.2).** Open question.
